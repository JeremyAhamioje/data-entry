Jeremy Ahamioje

Data Entry & Data Operations · Lagos, Nigeria

I work on the records side of a business: the intake queue, the export nobody wants to open, the folder of scans someone has been meaning to deal with. My job is to turn that into something clean, consistent, and usable.

I’ve done 3,000+ hours of data work on Upwork — data entry, web research, spreadsheet cleanup, verification, and PDF-to-spreadsheet conversion.

I’m also a developer. That matters less as a credential than as a habit: I think in structured data, I notice when a column is doing two jobs at once, and when a task is repeating itself I’ll usually write something small to handle it. The data cleaner on this desktop started exactly that way.

It also means I’m comfortable with the manual work. Plenty of data problems are too particular to automate, and the honest answer is to sit down and do them carefully.

Curious how I set up a job before starting? Open the notebook on the desktop.

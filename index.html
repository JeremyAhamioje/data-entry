Jeremy Ahamioje

Data Entry Specialist · 2026

I work in the part of a business nobody photographs: the intake queue, the reconciliation sheet, the folder of scans someone has been meaning to deal with since March.

My job is to make that pile boring. Consistent field formats, a documented rule for every judgement call, and a QA pass that catches the errors before a manager does. I care more about the audit trail than the keystroke count — though the keystrokes are fast too.

Most of my work is high-volume back-office: invoices, member rosters, claims batches, survey coding, vendor records. Tools change; the discipline doesn't. Everything I hand back is traceable to its source document.

When I'm not entering data I'm usually building small tools to avoid entering data — the data cleaner on this desktop started as a script for one messy address column.

Curious how I actually run a batch? Open the notebook on the desktop.
